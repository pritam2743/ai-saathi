AI should feel easier to explore.

AI Saathi is an independent discovery guide to help everyday users find AI tools for learning, writing, design, coding, research and productivity.

How we work

  • We organise tools by the task they can help with.
  • We link to official tool websites so you can check current features, pricing and terms.
  • We aim to publish useful guides, not promise that any tool is perfect.
