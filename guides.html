GUIDESIdeas start here
01 / STUDY

Use AI as a study partner, not a shortcut.

Ask an AI assistant to explain a difficult topic in simple language, then close the answer and explain it yourself.

  • Summarise your own notes.
  • Generate practice questions and solve them before checking answers.
  • Verify facts and formulas against trusted sources.
02 / CREATE

Turn a rough idea into a first draft.

Describe your audience, goal, tone and constraints. Ask for a few directions, choose what works, then edit it in your own voice.

  • Give clear context and examples.
  • Check content usage rights before publishing.
  • Protect private information.
03 / USE WISELY

Keep privacy and judgement in the loop.

AI can confidently produce errors. Avoid entering passwords, financial information or confidential details unless you understand the provider’s data practices.

  • Cross-check important answers.
  • Review privacy policy, pricing and terms.
  • Follow school, workplace and local rules.
